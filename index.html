Strategy & Architectural Breakdown

1. The Core Strategy

The bot applies a 15-period Simple Moving Average (SMA) momentum crossover on 1-minute BTC/USDT candles fetched live from Binance's public REST API.

  • Entry: Buy when the 1-minute close price crosses above the 15-period SMA.
  • Exit: Sell when the price drops below the SMA, or force-close at the final minute (Flat at the end).
  • Constraints Enforced: Minimum 5 completed trades, 0.1% fee per side, 0 BTC held at termination.

Why It Cannot Work (The 3 Structural Traps)

  1. The 0.2% Round-Trip Fee Friction: Every complete trade incurs a total fee penalty of ~0.2%. Over 5 to 8 mandatory trades, the bot must generate at least 1.0% to 1.6% in gross profit just to break even. Market noise systematically eats the account.
  2. Forced Liquidation (Flat at the end): The rule requiring 0 BTC balance at minute 1440 deprives the algorithm of mean-reversion recovery.
  3. No Look-Ahead & Overfitting Trap: Without future knowledge, lag-based indicators generate false signals in range-bound regimes.
